## Title patterns, lengths and visual styles

### Takeaway
Titles observed mix Hindi (Devanagari) and English, put the mantra + count first ("108 times / 108 बार"), and add a benefit or occasion tag (Monday Special, Mahashivratri, Ram Navami Special, Peace/Positive Energy/Meditation, Super Fast).

### Cited Findings
- Patterns seen in real titles: "[Mantra] 108 times | [Benefit] for Peace Positive Energy & Meditation" ([source](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4egcz2ch52I)); "महामृत्युंजय मंत्र 108 बार ... MONDAY SPECIAL MANTRA" ([source](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=YsrkW_p3eF0)); "Daily Chant!" ([source](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jao3TSC3bkk)); singer name in title ([Hariharan](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WiVJLFRbFSw)); meaning/subtitles in title ([Paudwal](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=L-y1sr1qUlE)); year tag "[2026]" ([Vedarth Music](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mpkpI_eUNvg)); duration in title "25 Minutes Non-Stop" and "30 Mins".
- Sound-design variants: "only vocals and ocean waves" ([source](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PVnitAxrY8A)), 432Hz ([source](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-aEgcRZLfpI)).
- Speed variants: "108 times in 3 minutes super fast" ([source](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xK2ekMrV3Dg)).

### Inferences
- Common length bands from titles: ~3 min (fast 108), ~25-30 min (meditation), plus 108-count standard (roughly 10-25 min depending on speed). Longer loops (1h/3h) not confirmed in results.

### Gaps
- Actual durations and thumbnail designs unseen.

## Festivals/days driving traffic and 6-month calendar from 30 Sep 2026

### Takeaway
Next six months contain the year's biggest devotional peak: Sharad Navratri (11-19/20 Oct), Dussehra (20 Oct), Diwali cluster (6-11 Nov), Chhath, Kartik, Dev Deepawali (24 Nov), then Gita Jayanti (Dec), Makar Sankranti, Vasant Panchami, Mahashivratri (6 Mar 2027) and Holi (22 Mar 2027). Traffic uplift by festival is NOT quantified by any source found; the weekday titles (Monday special) are evidence creators target weekday-deity pairings.

### Cited Findings
- Sharad Navratri: Ghatasthapana Sun 11 Oct 2026, ends with Vijayadashami (Dussehra) Tue 20 Oct 2026 — [Rampuri](https://rampuri.com/navratri-durga-puja-2016-dates/); [Dev Aastha](https://www.devaastha.com/blogs/festivals/navratri-9-colours-meaning-and-use) lists 11-19 Oct; conflicting: [HinduTone](https://hindutone.com/festivals/navratri-2026-sharad-navaratri-complete-guide-nri-diaspora/) title says Oct 2-11 (appears inconsistent with the others; verify locally).
- Pradosh Vrat: Thu 8 Oct, Fri 23 Oct, Fri 6 Nov 2026; Ravi Pradosh Sun 6 Dec — [DrRPSharma](https://drrpsharma.com/blog/pradosh-vrat-dates-2026-2027.html); Kartika Purnima 25 Nov, Ganadhipa Sankashti Mon 30 Nov, Utpanna Ekadashi Fri 4 Dec, Darsha Amavasya Tue 8 Dec (same search snippet set; index page [India TV](https://www.indiatvnews.com/lifestyle/spirituality/hindu-calendar-2026-month-wise-festivals-vrat-dates-january-december-2025-12-31-1023937)).
- Karva Chauth 29 Oct; Dhanteras 6 Nov; Choti Diwali 7 Nov; Diwali (Lakshmi Puja) 8 Nov; Govardhan Puja 10 Nov; Bhai Dooj 11 Nov; Chhath 13-16 Nov; Tulsi Vivah Sat 21 Nov (single search summary; see [Drik Panchang](https://www.drikpanchang.com/calendars/indian/indiancalendar.html), [Tulsi Vivah - Dev Aastha](https://www.devaastha.com/blogs/festivals/tulsi-vivah-2026-puja-vidhi-guide), [Khatu Wale Baba](https://khatuwalebaba.in/festival/tulsi-vivah/) which also says 21 Nov).
- Dev Deepawali (Kartik Purnima): 24 Nov 2026 per [search summary](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dev_Deepavali_(Varanasi)) sources; other snippet gives Kartika Purnima 25 Nov (purnima tithi spans both dates; Dev Deepawali is observed 24 Nov in some listings).
- Gita Jayanti: Sun 20 Dec 2026 per [ISKCON Bangalore](https://www.iskconbangalore.org/gita-jayanti/) and [BhaktiBharat](https://www.bhaktibharat.com/en/festival/gita-jayanti); one summary said 19 Dec (regional variance).

### Inferences
- Publish-ahead windows: Navratri/Durga mantra content by ~5 Oct; Lakshmi/Diwali mantras by ~30 Oct; Tulsi/Vishnu/Ekadashi (Dev Uthani ~ 20 Nov region, date not verified) mid-Nov; Gita/Krishna by early Dec; Shiva content peaks late Feb-6 Mar 2027 (Mahashivratri) and every Monday/Pradosh.

## New trends 2025-2026 (AI visuals, Shorts, live streams, Sanskrit, kids)

### Takeaway
AI devotional-video tutorials are proliferating in Hindi, but YouTube's July 2025 "inauthentic content" policy and 2026 enforcement make templated AI bhakti risky for monetisation. Live 24/7 japa streams exist.

### Cited Findings
- Hindi tutorials on making AI bhakti videos in 2026 ("copy paste") exist — [YouTube tutorial](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SfEXGmUSs4k).
- YouTube renamed "repetitious content" to "inauthentic content" on 15 Jul 2025; mass-produced/templated AI content ineligible, unique creative voice allowed — [TubeBuddy](https://www.tubebuddy.com/blog/youtube-ai-monetization-best-practices/), [ScaleLab](https://scalelab.com/en/why-youtube-is-cracking-down-on-ai-generated-content-in-2026), [Viddar](https://viddar.io/en/blog/youtube-inauthentic-content-policy-monetization).
- By Jan 2026 YouTube terminated 11 channels and wiped 6 others (35M subs, 4.7B lifetime views combined; not bhakti-specific) — as summarised by search result, sources above; TechCrunch 20 Jul 2026 on AI slop policy clarification: [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/07/20/youtube-clarifies-policies-around-ai-slop-and-upsetting-videos/) (not read in full).
- Youth engagement with bhakti via social media and digital platforms cited — [Bhaktiparv](https://bhaktiparv.com/bhakti-2025-latest/) (blog, low rigor).
- Live japa stream example: [LIVE Mantra Meditation, Hare Krishna](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zJ-zrcugMzc). Shorts devotional playlists exist: [Top Devotional Shorts](https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PLV_5eq7MC2L7G5f_IfD5EstwLFPhTO5bZ).

### Inferences
- A small channel should add original elements (own voice, real recitation, original visuals, meaning explanation) rather than pure AI slideshow loops.
