## Which Hindi bhakti videos crossed 100M / 500M / 1B, and what topics?

### Takeaway
One video dominates: "Shree Hanuman Chalisa" (Gulshan Kumar / Hariharan, T-Series Bhakti Sagar), the first devotional video past 1B views and now reported at 5B+, the most-viewed Indian video on YouTube. I found no reliable, sourced view counts for other bhakti videos (Shiv Tandav, Mahamrityunjay, Gayatri, Ganesh Aarti, Durga Chalisa, Krishna bhajans) in this session.

### Cited Findings (all APPROXIMATE, search snippets only)
- Hanuman Chalisa (Gulshan Kumar, Hariharan) is #1 most-viewed Indian song on YouTube with "over 5.75 billion views"; first hymn/bhajan past 4B; first devotional video past 1B — search summary citing [Wikipedia: most-viewed Indian YouTube videos](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_most-viewed_Indian_YouTube_videos) and [Songray Top 50](https://songray.in/most-viewed-indian-songs-on-youtube/) (not fetched; 5.75B figure unconfirmed and conflicts with the 5B+ title below)
- Video title itself claims "Most Viewed Devotional Song | 5 Billion+ Views" — [YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=EAcPNbtV7XA) (title seen in search result only)
- India.com headline (earlier date) claimed 4.6B views and 20M likes for Hanuman Chalisa/Hariharan — [India.com](https://www.india.com/entertainment/the-most-viewed-devotional-song-on-youtube-has-over-4-6-billion-views-has-20-million-likes-song-is-hanuman-chalisa-singer-is-hariharan-7885900/)
- Swarajya headline: "Three Billion Views For Hanuman Chalisa On T-Series' YouTube Channel" (older article, shows trajectory 3B -> 4.6B -> 5B+) — [Swarajya](https://swarajyamag.com/culture/three-billion-views-for-hanuman-chalisa-on-t-series-youtube-channel-how-hindu-devotional-songs-conquered-the-internet)
- Anuradha Paudwal "Om Jai Jagdish Hare" (Aartiyan channel upload, video song) shows about 4.8M views in a search summary; stats page at [kworb](https://kworb.net/youtube/video/3ucCEjXS9n8.html) (low confidence; other uploads of this aarti likely far larger)
- Many re-uploads of Hanuman Chalisa exist with titles like "Original Video ... 8K ... 2026", i.e. copycat/re-upload ecosystem — [YouTube search results](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5OiGH_8S-Cg)
- Multiple Shiv Tandav Stotram (Shankar Mahadevan) uploads exist across channels (Times Music Spiritual, others, "8D audio", "Devotional Visuals"); no view counts seen — [example](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=S980-z1qx3g)

### Inferences
- Hanuman Chalisa is an extreme outlier; the 100M+ tier for bhakti is likely populated by aartis, chalisas and Shiv/Krishna bhajans, but I cannot cite counts.
- 500M-1B tier counts unknown beyond Hanuman Chalisa.

### Gaps
- No verified per-video counts for Shiv Tandav, Mahamrityunjay (Suresh Wadkar 108 version exists: [YT Music](https://music.youtube.com/watch?v=SV1cVDhKZ6g)), Gayatri Mantra, Ganesh Aarti, Durga Chalisa, Krishna bhajans, Om Jai Jagdish Hare (top upload). Need Wikipedia list, Songray page, or kworb/Social Blade via a network that allows them.
- Upload years not confirmed (Hanuman Chalisa is commonly cited as uploaded ~2011 by T-Series; from memory, unsourced).

## Channels: biggest Hindi bhakti channels

### Takeaway
T-Series Bhakti Sagar is far ahead (about 79-80M subscribers); a second tier of 8-17M includes Bhajan Marg, Bhajan India, Spiritual Mantra, Saregama Bhakti.

### Cited Findings (APPROXIMATE)
- T-Series Bhakti Sagar: about 79M subscribers late 2025 (another summary says over 80M and 27th globally); 25,000+ videos, close to 28B views — [search summary of India.com/Essential Gyaan](https://essentialgyaan.com/top-devotional-bhakti-youtube-channels-in-india/); one snippet claiming "237 million subscribers" is wrong/conflicts and should be ignored. Channel: [YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/channel/UCaayLD9i5x4MmIoVZxXSv_g)
- Bhajan Marg (Premanand Maharaj-style Q&A/satsang, "Ekantik Vartalaap"): about 16.9-17.3M subscribers, +200K in 30 days, 156.55M views in 30 days, 7,359 videos, ranked ~#1,054 — [HypeAuditor](https://hypeauditor.com/youtube/UCEk1jBxAl6fe-_G37G7huQA/), [vidIQ](https://vidiq.com/youtube-stats/channel/UCEk1jBxAl6fe-_G37G7huQA/), [Social Blade](https://socialblade.com/youtube/handle/bhajanmarg)
- Bhajan India: 10M+ subscribers, billions of views — [Essential Gyaan](https://essentialgyaan.com/top-devotional-bhakti-youtube-channels-in-india/)
- Spiritual Mantra: 10M+ subscribers; Hari Om Bhakti: 2M+ — same source via [search summary](https://essentialgyaan.com/top-devotional-bhakti-youtube-channels-in-india/)
- Saregama Bhakti: about 7.7-8M subscribers, about 2B total views — [ThoughtLeaders](https://app.thoughtleaders.io/youtube/saregama-bhakti), [YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/@SaregamaBhakti)
- Shemaroo Bhakti: stats pages exist ([Social Blade](https://socialblade.com/youtube/channel/UC7ZivIYRB0fMSGh-THcTYbw), [vidIQ](https://vidiq.com/youtube-stats/channel/UC7ZivIYRB0fMSGh-THcTYbw/)) but numbers not obtained
- Zee Music Company (123.5M subs) is a general music channel, not bhakti-only — [search summary](https://hypeauditor.com/top-youtube-all-india/)

### Gaps
- No data obtained for Zee Music Bhakti, Rajshri Soul, Ashtvinayak, Anuradha Paudwal's own channel, Gulshan Kumar/Hindi Devotional/Spiritual Activity; per-channel top-video lists not obtained.

### Takeaway
Evidence is thin. Visible format patterns in titles: full-HD original video songs, lyrical videos with Hindi-English lyrics, live/looped aarti, "108 times" mantra tracks, 8D audio, "devotional visuals". Growth: Bhajan Marg grows via daily long-form satsang Q&A, not music.

### Cited Findings (APPROXIMATE)
- Title formats seen: "Aarti with Hindi English Lyrics ... LYRICAL VIDEO" ([YT](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NE3SWh9_vR4)); "Mahamrityunjaya Mantra 108 times" ([YT Music](https://music.youtube.com/watch?v=SV1cVDhKZ6g)); "8D AUDIO" Shiv Tandav ([YT](https://m.youtube.com/watch?v=BP0VyfkDDEI)); YouTube Shorts aartis ([Shorts](https://www.youtube.com/shorts/gTmt3cBEJ4Q)); live-stream aarti ([YT live](https://www.youtube.com/live/Q-CvXI3YbtE))

### Inferences
- Format dominance (lyrics, 1-hour loops, AI visuals) is plausible from titles but unquantified.
